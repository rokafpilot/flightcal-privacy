FlightCal Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026
Service provider: SmartPilots

This Privacy Policy explains how FlightCal ("the App") handles information when you use the App. FlightCal is an aviation operational support app designed for airline pilots and other aviation users. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

  • FlightCal does not require account sign-up or login.
  • FlightCal does not include advertising SDKs.
  • FlightCal does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Most settings and cached operational data are stored locally on your device for app functionality and offline convenience.
  • When you use weather, NOTAM, exchange-rate, or purchase-related features, limited data is transmitted to the relevant service provider as needed to deliver that feature.

2. Information Processed by the App

The App may process the following categories of information:

  • User-entered operational data, such as airport ICAO codes, calculator inputs, duty/rest values, unit conversion values, and currency conversion values.
  • App settings and preferences, such as theme and interface settings.
  • License and trial status data, such as trial start date and premium unlock status.
  • Cached operational content, such as cached weather, NOTAM, and exchange-rate data stored on the device to improve performance and offline usability.
  • Purchase status data returned by Google Play Billing in order to confirm entitlement to premium features.

3. How Information Is Used

The App uses information only for the following purposes:

  • To provide flight calculator, conversion, duty, weather, and NOTAM features.
  • To remember local app settings and improve usability across sessions.
  • To cache operational data for performance and limited offline use.
  • To validate trial status and premium entitlement.
  • To maintain app security, integrity, and service reliability.

4. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

FlightCal primarily stores certain data locally on your device using local app storage. This may include:

  • Theme and interface settings.
  • Trial start date and premium unlock flag.
  • Cached exchange rates.
  • Cached METAR, TAF, ATIS, and NOTAM results.

This local storage is used to make the App function properly and to reduce unnecessary repeated network requests.

5. Network Requests and Third-Party Services

When you use certain features, the App sends requests over the internet to third-party services or infrastructure necessary to provide that feature. These services may receive technical network information such as your IP address, request headers, or device/browser metadata as part of standard internet communication.

  • Weather and ATIS data providers, including aviation weather sources such as aviationweather.gov, atis.info, and metars.eu.
  • NOTAM proxy infrastructure, including SmartPilots-operated proxy infrastructure and upstream FAA/NOTAM-related services where required to deliver NOTAM data.
  • Exchange-rate providers, including open.er-api.com and open.exchangerate-api.com.
  • Google Play Billing, which processes purchase transactions and returns purchase status information needed for premium access.

FlightCal does not sell personal information and does not use data brokers. Information is shared only to the extent necessary to provide the requested feature.

6. In-App Purchases

FlightCal offers premium functionality through Google Play Billing. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play. FlightCal may receive limited purchase-related information, such as product identifiers, purchase status, and billing entitlement status, in order to unlock premium features. FlightCal does not receive or store your full payment card number.

7. Permissions

At the time of this policy, the Android app uses the following permissions:

  • INTERNET, to access weather, NOTAM, exchange-rate, and billing services.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING, to support Google Play in-app purchases.

8. Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device remains there until it expires, is overwritten, or is removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the App. Third-party services may retain their own server logs according to their respective policies.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to reduce unnecessary data collection and to use standard secure network connections where available. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

FlightCal is not directed to children and is intended for aviation operational use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International Access

Because the App is intended for global aviation use, this Privacy Policy page is intended to be publicly accessible worldwide without login or regional restrictions.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date on this page. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
